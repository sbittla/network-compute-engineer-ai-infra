## 3. Collection, screen and deduplication rules

- **Selection.** Board titles were filtered for network, HPC, GPU, fabric, cluster, supercompute,
  inference, SRE/reliability, performance and data-centre terms. Recruiting, sales, management, research,
  support-desk and construction-trade titles were dropped, and the rest were hand-picked to cover the six
  role families plus facilities and inference. The sample is **purposive, not random**.
- **Scope screen** (applied after reading each posting in full). A posting was kept only if its own text
  placed the role in AI/GPU/HPC data-centre infrastructure. Four were excluded: OpenAI "Network Engineer"
  (San Francisco and London; enterprise networking), Crusoe "Senior Staff Optics Engineer, Network
  Qualification & Strategy" and CoreWeave "Senior Optical Network Engineer II" (both WAN/long-haul
  optical).
- **Dedupe rule `ch24-role-v1`.** Two rows are one role when they share
  `role_key = casefold(employer) | normalised title`. Normalising the title casefolds it, collapses
  whitespace, strips a trailing location qualifier ("(Kansas City)", "- Memphis") and unifies "Staff+"
  with "Staff". Location, requisition ID and small differences in body wording do not create a new role.
  Different seniority bands in the title do. The kept row is the earliest collected row, because all rows
  share `date_checked` (the chapter's tie rule).
- **Merges (8 rows removed across 5 roles):** Lambda Data Center Operations System Engineer (4 rows →
  1: Kansas City, Salt Lake City, and two separate Plano requisitions); Together AI Senior or Staff
  Software Engineer, Inference / Compute Infrastructure Engineering (Amsterdam + India); Anthropic Staff(+)
  Infrastructure Engineer, Cluster Infrastructure (US + London); NVIDIA Senior HPC AI Cluster Engineer
  (Israel + US); NVIDIA Senior Solutions Architect, InfiniBand and Networking Ethernet - NVIS (India,
  Australia, Japan). Seven of the 8 removed rows are cross-posts for another location. One is a second
  requisition for the same site.
- **Not merged (borderline):** Together AI "(Staff / Senior Staff)" and "(Senior/Junior" AI Inference
  Reliability Engineer have the same body text but different level bands. Nscale "HPC Systems Engineer"
  and "Senior HPC Systems Engineer" have different bodies. Lambda Senior and Staff HPC Systems Architect
  have different bodies.
- **Seniority** comes from the title only, with `mixed` (two levels named) and `unspecified` (no level)
  added to the vocabulary. Years of experience were not converted to a level.
- **Skills** are short normalised tags that paraphrase each posting's required list (or its only list,
  e.g. "you might thrive if") and its preferred, bonus or "ways to stand out" list. One literal note:
  Graphcore's posting says "PDF, ECN and DCQCN". PFC was not inferred; the row records ECN, DCQCN and
  lossless Ethernet.
- **Not recorded:** salaries (deliberately), applicant counts, and anything beyond public posting text.

## 5. Limitations

- **Sample bias.** The sample is purposive, not random. Employers were chosen because their ATS exposes a
  public JSON feed (Greenhouse, Ashby, one Workday tenant), and titles were hand-picked to cover role
  families. Counts describe this sample, not the market. The 25 neocloud / 11 frontier-lab / 6 vendor
  split reflects that access route. Lambda (7 roles) and Nscale (6) are over-represented. Anthropic is one
  of the 11 employers; its rows were treated like every other employer's.
- **No hyperscaler.** AWS, Google, Meta, Microsoft and Oracle are absent: their careers sites were not
  reachable through a public feed here, and Microsoft's returned HTTP 429. Hyperscaler roles may weight
  skills differently, especially automation at scale and custom fabrics.
- **Vendor coverage is thin.** Only NVIDIA and Graphcore are present. Arista, Broadcom, AMD and Cisco
  were not collected.
- **Geography.** 36 of 42 roles list at least one US location. Only 6 are outside the US (Singapore,
  Amsterdam, India, Israel, Australia, Japan). Nothing is inferred about EU or APAC demand.
- **One-day window.** All rows were read on 2026-10-30, so the register shows nothing about trends,
  seasonality or time-to-fill. `first_published` ranges from 2025-10-16 to 2026-10-30. A long-open posting
  may be evergreen rather than a single vacancy.
- **Staleness.** Postings close without notice. Under the chapter's own 30-day rule the whole file is
  stale after **2026-11-29**. Re-check each URL before you cite a row, and re-collect rather than edit in
  place.
- **Interpretation.** Skill tags are the author's paraphrase. "Required" vs "preferred" is the employer's
  framing, and some postings have only a "you might thrive if" list, recorded as required. Frequency in
  postings is not scarcity or importance. Role-family assignment is a judgement call recorded per row
  (`role_family`).
- **Employer naming.** Rows record the employer name the board itself displayed on the access date (e.g.
  "SpaceXSI" on the board at slug `xai`). No corporate relationships are asserted.
