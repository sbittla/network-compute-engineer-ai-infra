**Nature of this review.** This is an AI-assisted specialist review (Claude, prompted as a GPU systems
reviewer). It is **not** an independent external human review and does not replace one before
publication.

**Date:** 2026-11-05

**Scope.** The published text of `concise/ch01.md` (AI infrastructure landscape), `concise/ch03.md`
(Linux host systems and diagnostics), `concise/ch04.md` (GPU architecture and CUDA execution),
`concise/ch05.md` (GPU interconnects and host topology) and `concise/ch06.md` (GPU performance
engineering). I cross-checked them against the matching `book/part-*/chNN-*/manuscript.md`,
`docs/REFERENCE_CLUSTER.md`, `docs/VERIFIED_FACTS.md`, Appendix A and Appendix E. Review areas:
evidence labels, recomputed models, H100/HGX generalization, ORION as illustrative, simulated-result

## Checks with no finding

- **Evidence labels.** Every worked model is labelled `[MODELED]` or `[SIMULATED]`, and the input values are `[ILLUSTRATIVE]` or `[VENDOR-SPEC, 2026-10]`. Ch 1 Step 7 says "None of these is a measurement". Ch 6's `[MEASURED]` items name the RTX 4060 Laptop result directory.
- **Simulated/modeled caveats.** Ch 5 §5.8 lists what the model omits (contention, spine congestion, algorithm choice, congestion control, correlations) and says to calibrate with nccl-tests. Ch 1 Table 1.3 states peak rate, no overlap and no recomputation. Ch 5 Table 5.6's CV note says it is iteration-level spread, not run-to-run.
- **Mermaid.** Fig 1.1 (data path, 8 rails + 4 spines, 1:1), Fig 1.2 (TTFT composition), Fig 3.1 (host data path), Fig 4.1 (two H2D ≈ 10 ms, kernel 0.27 ms, D2H ≈ 5 ms), Fig 5.1 (three GPU↔GPU paths, PCIe ≤ 63 GB/s) and Fig 5.2 (PIX GDR solid, host-staged dashed) all match the prose and the ORION topology.
- **Lab safety.** Ch 3 warns about `setpci` and forced retraining and says never to reboot or change BIOS with running ranks. Ch 4 says never to attach `ncu` to production and warns about the profiling-permission reload. Ch 5 warns that ACS/IOMMU are security controls. Ch 6 warns that `-lgc`, `-pl` and persistence mode are root and affect all tenants. Ch 1 says not to drain or change QoS or templates during diagnosis. Quoted mutating commands (`scontrol … drain`, `requeue`) appear only in "Safe change" steps.
- **Version-sensitive items consistent with Appendix E:** the `clocks_event_reasons` naming and bit 0x04, the DCGM 4.6 alias and 4.7 `GPU_UTIL` deprecation, the PyTorch NCCL PG timeout of 10 min (2.15), `P2P/CUMEM` lines (2.19+), the GDR gate defaulting to PXB, and `NCCL_PXN_DISABLE`.
