**C1 — 9.5.** No number or claim is wrong, and the worked example's arithmetic is exact. Two qualifiers were
lost in condensing. First, the full chapter says ORION-XL runs an NCCL 2.22-class build, which is *why*
`NCCL_IB_TIMEOUT` = 18 applies. Without that, a reader on NCCL ≥ 2.23 (default 20, so a budget about 4× longer,
≈ 34 s) would wrongly conclude that a 14 s flap hangs their job. Second, "Partition keys (P_Key) enforced by
switches" drops the full chapter's condition "strong for reachability *when switches enforce*". Neither is a
falsehood. The chapter also leaves an apparent contradiction unexplained (R ≈ 15 min against RTOs of 39–50 min,
see C3), which I score under pedagogy.

**C2 — 9.0.** The core mechanisms all survive: the failure-rate model, Young's interval, fault domains, FMEA and
its critique, ECC and row remapping, Xids, AER, link directionality, the retry budget, flight-recorder
localization, the hypothesis classes, containment, the async-checkpoint commit boundary, spares, re-packing, the
retry policy, the isolation table and containment exercises. Some decision-relevant material is gone.
(a) Table 19.4 drops the *host-side stall* row (slow node with *lower* GPU activity and high PSI), which is the
discriminator for the straggler row's "SM activity *higher*". It also drops the packet-loss/congestion row

lab's 39–50 min RTO. (e) The management-plane and secrets-prefix controls in 19.8 are cut. Most of these cost
breadth. Items (a) and (d) cost understanding.
