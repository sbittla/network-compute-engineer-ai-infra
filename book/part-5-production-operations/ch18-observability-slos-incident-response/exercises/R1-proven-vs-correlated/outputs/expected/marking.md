# Marking scheme: R1 Proven versus correlated in the lab postmortem

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 5 points

**Evidence required:** Concise written answers, each referencing a specific data file, metric, or section.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Proven: timing of leaf changes, scope covering both jobs, recovery on rollback (intervention) | 2 |
| Correlated only: the exact ECN → PFC mechanism (no queue-depth telemetry) | 2 |
| Evidence that would convert correlation to proof (watermarks, pause durations, controlled single-leaf reapplication) | 1 |
