# Marking scheme: K4 Why multiwindow burn-rate alerts need two windows

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** One concrete scenario for each window.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Long window prevents paging on short blips that spend a trivial share of the budget | 1 |
| Concrete long-window scenario with numbers (lab 7-minute deploy: 5 m burn 40, 1 h burn 4.65 < 14.4) | 1 |
| Short window prevents firing late / continuing after the fix; concrete scenario (problem stopped 10 min ago still pages on 1 h window) | 2 |
