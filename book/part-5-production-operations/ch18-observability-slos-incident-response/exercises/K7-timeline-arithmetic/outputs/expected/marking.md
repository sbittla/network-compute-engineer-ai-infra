# Marking scheme: K7 TTD, TTM, TTR and the onset bias

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** Three definitions; one sentence on the bias introduced by human-reported onset.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| TTD = detection − onset | 1 |
| TTM = mitigation (user impact stopped) − onset; TTR = resolution (all-clear) − onset | 2 |
| Bias: human-reported onset lags (48 min in INC-2291), understating TTD and impact and hiding the alerting gap | 1 |
