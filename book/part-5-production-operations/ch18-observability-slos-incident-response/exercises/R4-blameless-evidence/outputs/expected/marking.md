# Marking scheme: R4 What makes a postmortem blameless

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 5 points

**Evidence required:** Concise written answers, each referencing a specific data file, metric, or section.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Evidence of blamelessness: system conditions and decisions in context, no names on errors | 2 |
| Contributing factors rather than a single culprit; action items on systems with owners | 1 |
| A blaming sentence rewritten correctly in blameless form | 2 |
