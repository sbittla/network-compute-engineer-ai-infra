## 3. Upgrade planner (`plan_upgrade_waves`, target B-2026.10)

Excluded (10): n02, n18 `already_on_target`; n05 `state:draining`, n09 `state:drained`, n14 and n21
`state:repair`, n26 `state:discovered`, n29 `state:validated`, n31 `state:imaged`, n32

constraints (not only the reference ordering).

Discussion point: the planner runs on the *pre-replay* inventory. Ask learners what changes if it
runs after the replay (n03 is drained, n12 in repair, n09/n21/n29/n31/n32 schedulable) — the plan must
be recomputed immediately before each wave, not once at campaign start.
