# Marking scheme: K2 PCIe Gen 1 at idle: defect or power management?

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 5 points

**Evidence required:** Verdict with the mechanism (idle power management) and the condition that would indicate a defect.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Verdict: not a defect | 1 |
| Mechanism: link speed down-trains at idle to save power and returns to Gen 5 under load | 2 |
| Defect condition: below Gen 5 under load, or width below x16 (width does not change with load; x8 halves bandwidth to ≈ 31.5 GB/s per direction) | 2 |
