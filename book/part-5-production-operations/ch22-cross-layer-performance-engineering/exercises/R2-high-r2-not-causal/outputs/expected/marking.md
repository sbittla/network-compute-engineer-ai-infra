# Marking scheme: R2 Why a high R² is not evidence of causation

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** concise written answers, each naming the evidence (or missing evidence) it relies on (shared R1–R4 line).

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| R² measures in-sample fit, not direction of causation or omitted variables | 2 |
| Names omitted-variable/confounding or mechanism-absence as the gap | 1 |
| Uses the lab's evidence: the additive-only model also has R² ≈ 0.995 yet a spurious ECC effect | 1 |
