# Marking scheme: K5 Why the model needs a period indicator

Type knowledge-check · kind computed · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** the role of the term; a concrete misattribution it prevents.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Role: absorbs any shift common to all after-period jobs (e.g. container rebuild) | 2 |
| Concrete misattribution with lab numbers: dropping `after` inflates driver 0.90 -> 1.07 s, storage 1.40 -> 1.57 s, TP 8.96 -> 9.18 s | 1 |
| Explains the mechanism: the shift is pushed onto the most-exposed factors and the container change disappears from the report | 1 |
