# Marking scheme: K3 Mean versus median of per-job means

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** an interpretation of mean vs median; one concrete partition and the hypothesis it tests.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Interprets mean >> median as a minority of jobs badly hurt (placement-, node- or dataset-specific) | 1 |
| Interprets unchanged per-job p99 as a steady per-job slowdown, not intermittent stalls | 1 |
| Names one concrete partition available from records (e.g. TP group spans nodes; ran on upgraded nodes) | 1 |
| States the hypothesis the partition tests and its predicted outcome | 1 |
