# Chapter 20 — Exercise folders

verbatim), what to submit, and an empty `outputs/` for your evidence. Knowledge-check items (20-K*)
and reflection items (20-R*) are separate folders because they are marked separately.

and graded by the named checker lines; `written` = reasoning, design, or reflection.
