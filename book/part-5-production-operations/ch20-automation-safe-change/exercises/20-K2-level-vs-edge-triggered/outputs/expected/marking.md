# Marking scheme: 20-K2 Level- vs edge-triggered controllers

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** Written answer (2–5 sentences) that names the mechanism, not just the term.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Level-triggered acts on the current desired-vs-observed difference | 1 |
| Edge-triggered acts on events/changes | 1 |
| After a day down: edge-triggered missed events and never repairs drift; level-triggered converges | 1 |
