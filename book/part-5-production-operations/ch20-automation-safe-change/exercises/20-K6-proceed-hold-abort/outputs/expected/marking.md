# Marking scheme: 20-K6 Proceed, hold and abort

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** Written answer (2–5 sentences) that names the mechanism, not just the term.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Proceed: full bake evidence and no condition breached, with a concrete example | 1 |
| Hold: insufficient evidence (e.g. < 12 samples, telemetry gap), with a concrete example | 1 |
| Abort: pre-declared condition breached (e.g. median busbw drop > 5 %), with a concrete example | 1 |
| States that hold is not abort: no rollback, no progress, a human looks | 1 |
