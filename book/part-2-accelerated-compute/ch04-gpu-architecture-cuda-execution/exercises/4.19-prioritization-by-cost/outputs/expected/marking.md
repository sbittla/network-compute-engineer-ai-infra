# Marking scheme: 4.19 Prioritize by cost, not interest

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** short written answers (≤ 150 words each) that cite specific numbers or mechanisms.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Interest correlates with novelty, not with the time a finding costs | 1 |
| Worked-example numbers: low-occupancy kernel 2.43 ms vs pageable copy 8.77 ms (3.6× larger, one-line fix) | 1 |
| Better rule stated: rank by modeled saving × confidence / effort | 1 |
