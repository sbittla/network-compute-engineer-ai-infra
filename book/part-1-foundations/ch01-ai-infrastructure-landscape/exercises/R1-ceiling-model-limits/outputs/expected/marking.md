# Marking scheme: R1 What the ceiling model cannot capture

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** Three limitations of the Section 1.6 ceiling model, each with the measurement that would expose it.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| First limitation with a matching measurement (for example achievable vs peak rate, with GEMM or all_reduce_perf microbenchmarks) | 1 |
| Second limitation with a matching measurement (for example overlap, with an Nsight Systems timeline) | 1 |
| Third limitation with a matching measurement (for example latency, launch overhead, stragglers or contention, with per-rank timing or shared counters) | 1 |
