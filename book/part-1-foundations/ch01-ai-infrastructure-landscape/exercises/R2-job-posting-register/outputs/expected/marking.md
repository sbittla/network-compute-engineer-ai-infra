# Marking scheme: R2 A dated job-posting register

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** A register of at least 5 postings across at least 3 employer groups with the Section 1.8 fields, and a reasoned statement of what it can and cannot support.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Register has at least 5 postings from at least 3 employer groups, with every Section 1.8 field including the date checked | 2 |
| States what can be concluded (recurring skills in the sample on that date) and what cannot (market share, trends, completeness) | 1 |
| Explains why every market claim must be dated (postings change weekly, so an undated claim cannot be verified or refreshed) | 1 |
