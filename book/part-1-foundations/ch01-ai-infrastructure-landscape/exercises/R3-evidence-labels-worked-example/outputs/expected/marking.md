Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** The Section 1.9 numbers sorted into MODELED and ILLUSTRATIVE, and the list that must become MEASURED.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| MODELED numbers identified (FLOP counts, lower bounds, KV sizes, TP/DP volumes) | 1 |
