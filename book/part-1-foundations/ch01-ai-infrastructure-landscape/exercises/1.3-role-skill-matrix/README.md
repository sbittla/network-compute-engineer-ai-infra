# Exercise 1.3 — Role-to-skill matrix

**Difficulty:** ★ · **Tier:** L1 · **Type:** outline-mandated · **Kind:** written · **Points:** 10

## Prompt

<!-- prompt:start -->
Build a role-to-skill matrix for **AI Network Engineer**, **GPU Cluster Engineer**, and **AI
Infrastructure SRE** using the 0–3 scale of Section 1.8 and at least 12 skill domains. Then add a
fourth column for yourself today. For every cell where your score is ≥ 2 below the role you target,
name one chapter of this book and one concrete artifact (lab output, script, design doc) that would
close the gap.

**Evidence required:** the matrix; a written justification (one sentence) for each role's two
highest-rated and two lowest-rated domains; a gap list with chapter and artifact for each gap.
<!-- prompt:end -->

## Before you start

- **Chapter:** Chapter 1 of the book; every exercise prompt is also in [exercises.md](../../exercises.md).
- **Defaults:** Unless the prompt says otherwise, use the ORION values in [docs/REFERENCE_CLUSTER.md](../../../../../docs/REFERENCE_CLUSTER.md).
- **Checking your work:** Check your answer against the evidence the prompt asks for. After you have written it, compare it with the model answer and marking scheme in [outputs/expected/](outputs/expected/README.md); compare the reasoning and evidence, not the wording.

## What to submit

Put every deliverable in [`outputs/`](outputs/):

- `outputs/matrix.md`: the role-to-skill matrix with your own column
- `outputs/justification.md`: one sentence per role for its two highest and two lowest domains
- `outputs/gaps.md`: gap list: domain, gap size, chapter, artifact

Answer in prose. Give any number you use with its unit and an evidence label (`[MEASURED]`, `[MODELED]`, `[SIMULATED]` or `[ILLUSTRATIVE]`).
