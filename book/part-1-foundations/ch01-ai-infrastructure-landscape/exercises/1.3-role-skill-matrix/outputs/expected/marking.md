# Marking scheme: 1.3 Role-to-skill matrix

Type outline-mandated · kind written · tier L1 · 10 points

**Evidence required:** The matrix; a written justification (one sentence) for each role's two highest-rated and two lowest-rated domains; a gap list with chapter and artifact for each gap.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| At least 12 skill domains rated 0-3 for the three named roles, consistent with Table 1.10 within plus or minus 1 per cell | 3 |
| One-sentence justification for each role's two highest and two lowest domains | 3 |
| Personal column present and every gap of 2 or more mapped to a specific chapter | 2 |
| Each gap names a concrete artifact (lab output, script, design doc) | 2 |
