# Marking scheme: Q2 A profile that exceeds NIC line rate

Type knowledge-check · kind computed · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** The implied rate against line rate, and the conclusion with plausible causes.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Implied rate 40 GB / 0.5 s = 80 GB/s computed with units | 1 |
| Compared with 400 Gb/s = 50 GB/s line rate and declared impossible (ceiling fraction 1.6) | 1 |
| At least two plausible data causes (TX + RX summed, two NICs aggregated, bits/bytes confusion, wrong time window) | 1 |
