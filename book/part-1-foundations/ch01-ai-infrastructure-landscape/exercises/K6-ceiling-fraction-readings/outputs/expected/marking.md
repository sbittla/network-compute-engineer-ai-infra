# Marking scheme: K6 Reading a ceiling fraction

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** Interpretation of a ceiling fraction above 1.0 and of 0.1.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Above 1.0 is physically impossible and means a data error, with at least two plausible causes (units, bidirectional counter, double count, wrong time) | 2 |
| 0.1 means no throughput ceiling explains the time | 1 |
| Names what does dominate at 0.1 (latency, launch overhead, synchronization, queueing or stragglers) | 1 |
