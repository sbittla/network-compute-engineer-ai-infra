# Marking scheme: 2.1.7 Allocation, MFU and goodput

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** Definitions of the three utilizations and why they multiply.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Allocation is the fraction of GPU-hours given to jobs | 1 |
| MFU is useful model FLOP/s divided by peak FLOP/s while running; goodput is the fraction of allocated time that becomes durable progress | 1 |
| They multiply because each applies to what remains after the previous one (with an example such as 0.80 x 0.38 x 0.92 = 0.28) | 1 |
