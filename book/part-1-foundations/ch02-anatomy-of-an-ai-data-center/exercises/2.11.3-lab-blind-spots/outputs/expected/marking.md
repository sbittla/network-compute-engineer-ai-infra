# Marking scheme: 2.11.3 What the lab cannot tell you

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** One paragraph listing dynamic and physical effects the static model cannot capture.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Names at least three effects outside a static spreadsheet model (thermal transients, CDU control dynamics, PSU behaviour, breaker trip curves, real coolant performance, workload power profiles) | 1 |
| Explains why each matters for running well, not just fitting | 1 |
| Names how the gap would be closed (commissioning, burn-in, measured profiles) | 1 |
