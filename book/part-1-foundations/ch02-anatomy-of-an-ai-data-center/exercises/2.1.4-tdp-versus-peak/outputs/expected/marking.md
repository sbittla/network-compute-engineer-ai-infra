# Marking scheme: 2.1.4 TDP versus peak power

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** Written answer distinguishing TDP from peak draw and naming the absorbing stages.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| TDP is the sustained design power and default power limit; instantaneous draw can exceed it for microseconds to milliseconds | 1 |
| Microsecond excursions are absorbed by board capacitors, voltage regulators and PSU hold-up | 1 |
| Step-boundary swings (tens of ms to seconds) are seen by PSUs, PDUs, breakers and the UPS | 1 |
