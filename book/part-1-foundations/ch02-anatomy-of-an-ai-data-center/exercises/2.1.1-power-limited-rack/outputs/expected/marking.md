# Marking scheme: 2.1.1 Why an ORION rack is power-limited, not space-limited

Type knowledge-check · kind computed · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** Written answer with a space number and a power number, formula to result.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Space number given (4 nodes use 32U; about 13U free, so a fifth 8U node fits physically) | 1 |
| Power number given (41.0 kW maximum at 4 nodes; 51.2 kW and 56.3 kW per side with margin at 5 nodes) | 1 |
| Names what the fifth node breaks (per-rack coolant, CDU or floor design) even though 65.55 kW per side is available | 1 |
