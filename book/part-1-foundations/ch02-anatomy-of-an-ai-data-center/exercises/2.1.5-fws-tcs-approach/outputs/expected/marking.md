# Marking scheme: 2.1.5 FWS, TCS and approach temperature

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** Definitions of FWS, TCS and approach, and the hot-afternoon mechanism.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| FWS is the facility primary water loop; TCS is the clean, filtered secondary loop through the cold plates | 1 |
| Approach is TCS supply minus FWS supply at the CDU heat exchanger | 1 |
| Hot afternoon raises FWS supply, TCS supply follows at roughly constant approach, cold plates warm, and a GPU with little margin enters thermal slowdown | 1 |
