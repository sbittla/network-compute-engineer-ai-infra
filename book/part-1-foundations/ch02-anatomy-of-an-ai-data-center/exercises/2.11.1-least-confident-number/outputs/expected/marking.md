# Marking scheme: 2.11.1 The least confident number in the fit assessment

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** One paragraph naming the number, why it is uncertain, and the measurement (tier and command) that would replace it.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Names a specific number from Section 2.10 (for example the 10 % transient margin, the 75 % capture fraction or the 10.2 kW node maximum) | 1 |
| Explains why it is the least certain and what depends on it | 1 |
| Proposes a measurement with its tier and command (L2 BMC/DCMI and nvidia-smi sampling under burn-in, or L3 rack/CDU metering) | 1 |
