# Marking scheme: 2.1.8 Why the optics count matters operationally

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** Two operational reasons, each explained.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| First reason explained (for example optics are frequently failing parts, so the count drives spares stock and repair workload) | 1 |
| Second reason explained (for example optic power and cooling, or reach constraining rack placement) | 1 |
| Reasons are operational rather than purchase cost, with an ORION number (1,024 single-port transceivers) | 1 |
