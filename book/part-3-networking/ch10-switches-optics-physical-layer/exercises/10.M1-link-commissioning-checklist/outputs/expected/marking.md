# Marking scheme: 10.M1 Link commissioning checklist

Type outline-mandated · kind written · tier L1 · 10 points

**Evidence required:** a checklist table with all six required areas present, numeric pass criteria for optical power, lane health, and error counters, and a stated soak duration.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| All six required areas present (port config, transceiver compatibility, optical power, lane health, error counters, cable labelling), 1 point each | 6 |
| Numeric pass criteria for optical power, lane health and error counters, plus a stated soak duration (≥ 30 min loaded) | 2 |
| A failure action for every item, with acceptance thresholds stricter than alert thresholds | 2 |
