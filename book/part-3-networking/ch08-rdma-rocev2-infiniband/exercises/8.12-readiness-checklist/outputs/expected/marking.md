# Marking scheme: 8.12 Pre-deployment readiness checklist

Type outline-mandated · kind written · tier L1 · 20 points

**Evidence required:** ≥ 15 items with command, criterion, read-only flag, automation flag; switch-side items identified with an evidence source; ordering justified.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Covers NIC, firmware, driver, addressing, MTU and peer validation (plus QoS and GPUDirect RDMA) | 8 |
| Criteria are precise and testable, with real commands | 6 |
| Read-only and automated-in-lab flags correct for every item | 3 |
| Switch-side items identified with an evidence source; ordering justified | 3 |
