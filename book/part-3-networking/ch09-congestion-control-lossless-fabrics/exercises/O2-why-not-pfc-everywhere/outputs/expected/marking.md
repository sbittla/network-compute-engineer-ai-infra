# Marking scheme: O2 Why not PFC everywhere?

Type outline-mandated · kind written · tier L1 · 15 points

**Evidence required:** at least four distinct risks, each with its mechanism; a procedure of at least six steps naming the counters read at each step and the decision rule applied.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Risk 1 with mechanism (e.g. HOL blocking / victim flows) | 2 |
| Risk 2 with mechanism (e.g. pause propagation / congestion trees) | 2 |
| Risk 3 with mechanism (e.g. PFC storms without a watchdog) | 2 |
| Risk 4 with mechanism (e.g. deadlock, buffer cost, masking of ECN misconfiguration) | 2 |
| Numbered procedure of ≥ 6 steps, each naming the counters read and the decision rule | 7 |
