# Marking scheme: O3 Design a controlled congestion experiment

Type outline-mandated · kind written · tier L1 · 20 points

**Evidence required:** hypothesis; both configurations in full; workload; one controlled variable; repetitions (≥ 5) and warm-up; metrics; analysis method; environment manifest fields; safety boundary; a "cannot conclude" list of at least four items specific to the model.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Falsifiable hypothesis and both configurations in full, differing in one controlled variable | 4 |
| Workload, warm-up, ≥ 5 interleaved repetitions | 4 |
| Metrics and counters, and an analysis method (median, spread, decision rule) | 4 |
| Environment manifest fields and the safety boundary | 4 |
| 'Cannot conclude' list of ≥ 4 model-specific items, plus what the experiment would and would not establish | 4 |
