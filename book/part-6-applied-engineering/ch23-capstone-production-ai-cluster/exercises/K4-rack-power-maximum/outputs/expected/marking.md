# Marking scheme: K4 Rack power budgets use maximum node power

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 6 points

**Evidence required:** the reason plus two events; the planned budget and node count with arithmetic

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Reason: the rack budget is a hard electrical limit (breaker/PDU rating) that trips on the worst case, not the average | 2 |
| First operational event that drives a rack toward maximum, correctly explained | 1 |
| Second, distinct operational event, correctly explained | 1 |
| Planned budget 50 x 0.9 = 45 kW and floor(45 / 10.2) = 4 nodes, arithmetic shown | 2 |
