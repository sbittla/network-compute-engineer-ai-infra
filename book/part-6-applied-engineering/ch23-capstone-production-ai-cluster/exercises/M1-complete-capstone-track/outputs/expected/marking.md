**Evidence required:** every required artifact listed in the chosen brief; a design bundle that lints with gate PASS (or with each remaining finding explicitly waived with a reason); evidence labels on every number

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Scored with capstones/RUBRIC.md: total ≥ 70/100 to pass, with R2 ≥ 10/20 and R4 ≥ 10/20 | 60 |

| Design bundle lints with gate PASS, or each remaining finding waived with a reason | 15 |
| Every number carries an evidence label; no MEASURED physical claim from L1 (R4 cap) | 10 |
