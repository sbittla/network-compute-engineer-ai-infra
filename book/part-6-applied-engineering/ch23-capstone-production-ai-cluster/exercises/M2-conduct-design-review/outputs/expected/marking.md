# Marking scheme: M2 Conduct a design review

Type outline-mandated · kind written · tier L1 · 20 points

**Evidence required:** pre-read bundle, linter report, written findings with severities, the meeting outcome, conditions with owners and dates, and the updated bundle

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| At least two reviewers who did not write the design; pre-read bundle and linter report circulated | 4 |
| Findings written before the meeting, with severities, covering assumptions, ADRs, failure domains and operations (Table 23.7) | 6 |
| Explicit outcome (approved / approved with conditions / rework); contested findings discussed | 4 |
| Conditions with owners and dates, and evidence (diff or changelog) that the bundle was updated | 6 |
