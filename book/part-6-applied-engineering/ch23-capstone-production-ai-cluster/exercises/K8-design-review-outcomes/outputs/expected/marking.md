# Marking scheme: K8 The three outcomes of a design review

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** three outcomes; one sentence on accountability

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Approved | 1 |
| Approved with conditions, each condition with an owner and a date | 1 |
| Rework (another review required) | 1 |
| Accountability: 'take it offline' has no owner, date or decision | 1 |
