# Marking scheme: K3 Senior versus staff

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 6 points

**Evidence required:** two short paragraphs; at least two outcomes per level, phrased as verifiable results.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Senior response: leads the incident, proves cause with a discriminating test, postmortem names the detection gap | 2 |
| Staff response: recognizes the cross-incident pattern and drives a multi-team policy/telemetry change | 2 |
| At least two verifiable outcomes per level (results, not activities) | 2 |
