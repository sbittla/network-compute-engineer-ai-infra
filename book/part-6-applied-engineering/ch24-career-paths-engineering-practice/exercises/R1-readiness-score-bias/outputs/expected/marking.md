# Marking scheme: R1 Readiness score bias

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** 100–200 words naming specific evidence rather than general intent (shared R1–R4 line).

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| First systematic error with its direction (e.g. self-report over-claiming) | 1 |
| External evidence that corrects the first error | 1 |
| Second, distinct systematic error (matrix/ladder mismatch or stale evidence) | 1 |
| External evidence that corrects the second error | 1 |
