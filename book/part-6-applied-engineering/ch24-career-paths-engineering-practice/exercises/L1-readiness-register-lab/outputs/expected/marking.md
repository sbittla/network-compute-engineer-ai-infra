Type hands-on-lab · kind lab · tier L1 · 100 points

**Evidence required:** `python check.py` output showing 12/12 PASS; the generated report; a one-page explanation of the flags (rule, why it exists, what the learner should do).

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Tasks 1–4 (Part A: evidence rules, levels, readiness, gap plan) pass check.py | 30 |
| Tasks 5–8 (Part B: URL/key, field rules, duplicate clusters, validation and skill frequency) pass check.py | 30 |
| Written explanation (≤ 1 page) of every evidence flag for the sample learner and why each rule exists | 10 |
| Own role-readiness matrix (target plus two adjacent roles), each level backed by a named artifact | 15 |
| Own gap plan: top three gaps, the artifact for each, and a date | 10 |
| Statement of limitations: what the score can and cannot say about hiring readiness | 5 |
