# Marking scheme: D1 A 12-week plan from "near" to "ready"

Type design-challenge · kind written · tier L1 · 15 points

**Evidence required:** current matrix; plan with dated artifacts and hours; projected score and status after each artifact; one paragraph on what you are *not* doing and why.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Current matrix produced by the lab on the learner's real evidence | 3 |
| Projected score and status after each artifact, with arithmetic | 4 |
| Plan fits ≤ 10 h/week with realistic, dated hours | 2 |
| Depth/breadth and L1/L2 choice argued from the evidence caps | 3 |
| Honest 'not doing' paragraph | 3 |
