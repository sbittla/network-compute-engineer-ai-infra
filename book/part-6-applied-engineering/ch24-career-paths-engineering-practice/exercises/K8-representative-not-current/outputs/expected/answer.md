# K8 — Representative, not current: model answer

Source: Section 24.4 and the caption of Table 24.7.

Hiring changes month to month, and Table 24.7's organizations come from the course outline's grouping of
employers by the *engineering problems they hire for*; the book has not verified that any of them has an
open role with a given title today. Dropping the qualification would turn a curriculum synthesis into an
implied factual claim about current openings, the kind of claim Section 24.4 says needs a dated,
deduplicated register. A reader could then target an employer believing a role exists, or cite the table
as market evidence ("frontier labs are hiring AI network engineers") when it is not.

**Why the distinction is checkable here.** The real register in `lab/data/job_register_2026-10.csv` is
labelled `[OBSERVED, 2026-10-30]`, covers 11 employers with no hyperscaler, and is stale after 2026-11-29.
Even that dated evidence supports only "these roles were open on that date", which is why the table keeps
"representative" in its caption.

**Common mistakes.** Answering "for legal reasons" without the evidence argument; claiming the table is
wrong rather than unverified.
