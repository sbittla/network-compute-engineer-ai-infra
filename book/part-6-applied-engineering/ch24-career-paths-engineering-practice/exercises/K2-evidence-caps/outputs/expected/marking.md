# Marking scheme: K2 Evidence caps

Type knowledge-check · kind written · tier L1 · 6 points

**Evidence required:** one paragraph per level boundary, each naming the observable behaviour that changes.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Boundary 1→2: a course shows vocabulary only; an L1 lab shows guided reproduction of reasoning | 2 |
| Boundary 2→3: independent framing, design or diagnosis (capstone, hardware lab with manifest, published case study) | 2 |
| Boundary 3→4: production ownership verified by someone else | 2 |
