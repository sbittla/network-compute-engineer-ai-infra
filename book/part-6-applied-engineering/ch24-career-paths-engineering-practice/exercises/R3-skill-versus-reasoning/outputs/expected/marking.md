# Marking scheme: R3 Skill versus reasoning evidence

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 4 points

**Evidence required:** 100–200 words naming specific evidence rather than general intent (shared R1–R4 line).

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| States the line: number from your own environment with a manifest and repetitions vs from a model or supplied data | 2 |
| Concrete example from each side drawn from the learner's labs | 1 |
| Notes labels must match (`[MEASURED]` only for the first; simulation-as-measurement is an integrity failure) | 1 |
