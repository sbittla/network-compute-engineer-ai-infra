# Marking scheme: R3 When to re-evaluate disaggregation

Type reflection · kind written · tier L1 · 3 points

**Evidence required:** the traffic change, the leading metric, and the threshold you would set.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Traffic change: growth in prompt tokens per request or the prompt:output ratio (e.g. RAG adoption) | 1 |
| Leading metric: ρ_prefill (prefill share of iterations) and ITL p99 rising before TTFT breaches | 1 |
| A concrete threshold (e.g. ρ_prefill > 0.55 or ITL p99 > 2x baseline sustained) | 1 |
